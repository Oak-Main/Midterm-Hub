# Kansas election-day polling hours correction

Checked November 3, 2026 UTC. The October 20 edition displayed 7 a.m. to
7 p.m. as the Kansas statewide hours, with a separate warning that county
schedules and time zones could differ. That presentation did not distinguish
the statutory defaults for the two time zones.

- [Kansas Statutes 25-106](https://www.kslegislature.gov/b2025_26/laws/025_000_0000_chapter/025_001_0000_article/025_001_0006_section/025_001_0006_k/) sets a 7 a.m.–7 p.m. default in Central-time counties and a 6 a.m.–6 p.m. default in Mountain-time counties. County election officers may set and announce other hours within the limits in the statute.
- The [Kansas Secretary of State's voter information](https://www.sos.ks.gov/elections/voter-information.html) describes the general 7 a.m.–7 p.m. rule and possible county variations, and tells voters to verify their county's hours. Its summary does not specify the Mountain-time default.

The state guide, national table, JSON, CSV and calendar download now state these as defaults and
direct readers to their county election officer for the actual schedule. The
earlier value is retained as superseded in the JSON research record. This is a
targeted correction; the research edition's remaining source dates are unchanged.
