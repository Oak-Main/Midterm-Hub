Election Day questions

Common questions, answered first

When is Election Day 2026?

Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026 — the general election in Texas is held that day.

What time do polls close in Texas?

Texas polls close 8:00 p.m. ET (central time zone); 9:00 p.m. ET (mountain time zone).

Where are Texas 2026 results?

The Texas Secretary of State publishes official results. Texas poll-closing times and the official results page → This site does not publish vote counts or call races.
